About

I am presently a Urology Senior Resident in the Department of Urology at Kasturba Medical College, Manipal, with a foundation in general surgery, active involvement in clinical research, and a consistent academic record.

My postgraduate thesis compares suction versus non-suction access sheaths in retrograde intrarenal surgery (RIRS), focusing on intraoperative visibility and pressure dynamics. My broader interests span evidence-based urology, surgical innovation, artificial intelligence in medicine, and digital health platforms.
