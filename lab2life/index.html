About this ledger. A discovery date is the year a finding was first reported. The “first major application” is the earliest documented use at meaningful scale that did real work for people outside a research setting. Smaller or niche earlier uses are real and are noted under “Why these dates?” on each story, because where to draw this line is a judgement call. “Years waited” is the gap between the two and is approximate. “At the time” notes summarise how contemporaries reacted and are not quotations. Headlines are verbatim, checked against live sources, and shown only where one could be found. Stories tagged “not yet fact-checked” have not had a separate source check. Horizon ratings are editorial judgements, not forecasts. Corrections are welcome. Entries marked added came from viewers of this page and have not been edited.
